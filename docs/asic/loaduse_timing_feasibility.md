# Load-use timing feasibility

The conceptual cone is synchronous SRAM → bank mux → way mux → hit/data selection → LSU formatting → bypass mux → dependent EX operand. Qualified SRAM C2Q values were approximately 0.229–0.324 ns. Before implementation, risk was classified MODERATE at 500 MHz and HIGH at 666/740 MHz, justifying a P1 RTL experiment. This was a feasibility judgement, not timing-closure proof.
