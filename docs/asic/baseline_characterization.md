# Baseline characterization

The 5 ns pilot passed and qualified the synthesis/link/constraint methodology. The controlled sweep then characterized 4.5 through 1.0 ns. Baseline 2.0 ns passed with +0.000188589 ns WNS. The 1.35 ns stress point passed with +0.0000474453 ns WNS, while 1.30 ns failed with -0.00990212 ns WNS, -1.13592207 ns TNS, and 149 violating paths. The 740.7 MHz result is a known pre-layout stress-pass point, not product or silicon Fmax.
